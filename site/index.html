A quiet place to write, next to your AI agents

Quillwright is a desktop writing app for novels and long documents. Your book is a folder of plain Markdown files. The AI tools you already use can work on it while you write, and nothing reaches your text without your approval.

Linux · Windows · macOS

Why Quillwright

Made for writers who work with AI, not for AI that replaces writers.

i.

Your book stays yours

No proprietary format and no cloud. One Markdown file per chapter and per character, readable in any editor, easy to back up, and ready for Git.

ii.

Works with the agents you use

Claude Code, Codex, or a local model through Ollama. Quillwright understands chapters and characters, and the agents work on the same folder you do.

iii.

One small app

A single native program, written in Rust. No account, no subscription, no API keys. Export to Word, EPUB, and PDF is built in.

Writing with agents

Agents edit the files. You stay in charge.

  • Changes appear as they happenWhen an agent adds a chapter or revises the open one, the sidebar and the page update, and you keep your place.
  • Suggestions are diffs, not surprisesRewrite a passage, continue a scene, or check a chapter against your world rules. You see every change and accept it or not.
  • Your words are never overwrittenAtomic saves, a check before every write, and a version history kept outside the project.

Works with Claude Code, Codex, Aider, Ollama, or any command-line tool.

Features

Everything a long project needs, and nothing in the way.

A page, not a text box

Live Markdown styling in a serif typeface on a centered column. The syntax only shows on the line you are editing.

Manuscript sidebar

Chapters in reading order with word counts, the chapter's outline, characters, and notes. Add the next chapter with one click.

Focus tools

Full-screen focus mode, typewriter scrolling, dimmed paragraphs, and Paper, Sepia, and Night themes.

Research beside the page

PDFs, images, and notes open next to your chapter. Search inside PDFs and send a passage to the assistant with its source.

Search the whole project

Chapters, character sheets, notes, and PDFs, ignoring case and accents.

In your language

The app in English, Portuguese, Spanish, or Chinese. Spell checking in your book's language, and character names are known automatically.

Autosave and history

Saved a moment after you stop typing. Earlier versions of each chapter can be compared and restored.

Export, offline

Word, EPUB, PDF, OpenDocument, and HTML, with a title page and a table of contents. Nothing else to install.

Progress you can see

Words in the chapter and the book, reading time, words written this session, and an optional goal.

Project templates

Start with a structure that agents understand.

New Project creates the folders and a guide, AGENT.md, that tells AI agents how to work on the project. Or open any folder as it is.

Novel

Chapters, character sheets, and a document for the rules of your world. The assistant checks continuity against them.

my-novel/ ├── AGENT.md ├── world-rules.md ├── chapters/ │ └── 001-opening.md └── characters/ └── example-character.md

Legal case

Filings, parties, facts with their evidence, and a timeline. The assistant never invents facts or citations. In English or Portuguese.

case/ ├── AGENT.md ├── facts.md · timeline.md · evidence.md ├── filings/ │ └── 001-complaint.md ├── parties/ └── case-files/ court PDFs

Open source

Open source today, and always.

Quillwright is released under the MIT license, and it will stay open source. Read the code, build it yourself, change it, and share it. Your books are plain files, so you can always leave with everything.
